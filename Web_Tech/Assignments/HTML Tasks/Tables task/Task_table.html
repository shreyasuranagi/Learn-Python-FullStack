Types of clouds

Cirrus.
delicate wispy clouds

Cirrocumulus.
Patchy sheet of ice crystals

Cirrostatus.
veil or foggy appearance
near the sun.

Altocumulus.
mid-level layered clouds

Altostratus.
blue-gray mid-level clouds
cause light rain

Nimbostratus.
dark, gray clouds that bring
steady precipitation

Cumulus.
fluffy, puffy clouds
on clear days

Cumulonimbus.
heavy and dense
thunderclouds

Stratocumulus.
low-level, lumpy clouds
